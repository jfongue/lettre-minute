Letter Minute

Privacy policy · effective 25 September 2026

In short: you play without an account; creating one is optional. Nothing is sold. In the app, a short ad follows each category pick from the second one on; that is the only moment an ad network, Google, receives any data. The game keeps your scores and your words to run the leaderboard and the rarity bonus, and you can erase everything from the menu.

What stays on your device

Your level, your XP, your records and the words you have suggested are saved in the app’s local storage. They leave the device only for the syncing described below.

What the server keeps

The first time you open it, the game gives you an anonymous identifier: no name, no email address and no phone number is asked for to play.

If you create an account, to pick up your progress on another device, appear on the leaderboards or play with friends, it adds the player name you choose, your email address and your password, kept only in encrypted form. With Google sign-in, Google passes your email address on to us; we receive neither your Google password nor your contacts. The address is used only to sign you in and to send you a code if you forget your password: never a newsletter, never advertising.

Kept under your identifier:

  • your games: score, number of words, best streak, number of skips, date;
  • the words found in each game, used to measure which words everyone writes;
  • your progress: XP, number of games, records;
  • the words you suggest to complete the dictionary;
  • your avatar and, if you moderate, your votes on the suggested words;
  • your friends: requests sent, received and accepted;
  • challenges between friends: who is invited, and each player’s game (score and words), visible to the challenge’s other players;
  • your phone’s notification token, if you accept notifications: it is used only to tell you about a challenge received or finished, and it is erased when you sign out.

What other players see: only named accounts appear on the leaderboards, under their player name and avatar, with their score; a player without an account does not appear there. Your friends see your name, your avatar and your score for the week, and the players in a challenge see each other’s words in that challenge. Your email address is never shown to anyone. Outside challenges, words played are never shown with the identity of whoever wrote them.

Why

To make the game work, and nothing else: the leaderboard, the rarity bonus (a word everyone writes earns less), friends and challenges, and adding the words players suggest to the dictionary. The legal basis is the performance of the service you use.

Who has access

The data is hosted by Supabase, which acts as a processor and does not use it for its own purposes. No data is sold, rented or shared with advertisers: your games, your words and your game identifier are never passed on to the ad network. Notifications go through Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google), which receives only the phone’s token and the text of the message.

Advertising

From the second category pick on, a short ad is shown after that pick. It is served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited), which acts as a separate controller. To choose the ad, measure its display and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect the device’s advertising ID, the IP address (from which an approximate location is inferred), information about the device and about your interaction with the ad.

In the European Union, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a form asks for your consent before the first ad; without it, ads are not personalised. You can change your mind at any time: Menu › Options › Ad choices. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in the phone’s Google settings. How Google processes this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The web version of the game shows no ads.

Erase your data

In the game: Menu › Profile › Erase my data, then Erase everything. Your account (name, email address, password), your games, your words played, your friends and your suggestions are deleted from the server immediately, and your progress is reset on the device. Only a word that has already entered the dictionary stays there, with no link to you.

Uninstalling the app erases the data on the device, but not the data on the server: erase it from the game first, or write to us.

Retention

Until you erase it, from the game or simply by asking.

Children

The game suits all ages and is played without any personal information; it is not aimed specifically at children. Creating an account requires an email address: in France, under 15, a parent’s consent is needed.

Your rights and contact

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure and objection (GDPR). To exercise them, or for any question: fongue.jeremy@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (the French data protection authority).
