Lettre Minute

Privacy policy · effective 24 September 2026

In short: no account to create, nothing sold. A short ad follows each category pick from the second one on; it is the only time a third party, Google, receives data. The game keeps your scores and your words to run the leaderboard and the rarity bonus, and you can erase everything from the home screen.

What stays on your device

Your level, your XP, your records and the words you have suggested are saved in the app’s local storage. They only leave the device for the sync described below.

What the server keeps

The first time you open it, the game gives you an anonymous identifier: no name, no email address and no phone number is asked for. Kept under this identifier are:

  • your games: score, number of words, best streak, number of skips, date;
  • the words found in each game, used to measure which words everyone writes;
  • your progress: XP, number of games, records;
  • the words you suggest to complete the dictionary.

The leaderboard shows each player’s best score under the name “Anonymous”. Words played are never shown alongside the identity of the person who wrote them.

Why

To make the game work, and nothing else: the leaderboard, the rarity bonus (a word everyone writes earns less) and adding to the dictionary the words requested by several players. The legal basis is the performance of the service you are using.

Who has access

The data is hosted by Supabase, which acts as a processor and does not use it for its own purposes. No data is sold, rented or shared with advertisers: your games, your words and your game identifier are never passed to the ad network.

Advertising

From the second category pick on, a short ad is shown after that pick. It is provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited), which acts as a separate controller. To choose the ad, measure its display and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect the device’s advertising ID, the IP address (from which an approximate location is inferred), information about the device and about your interaction with the ad.

In the European Union, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a form asks for your consent before the first ad; without it, ads are not personalised. You can change your mind at any time: Menu › Options › Ad choices. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in the phone’s Google settings. How Google processes this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The web version of the game shows no ads.

Erasing your data

On the home screen, at the bottom: Erase my data, then Erase everything. Your identifier, your games, your words played and your suggestions are deleted from the server immediately, and your progress is reset on the device. Only a word that has already joined the dictionary stays there, with no link to you.

Uninstalling the app erases the data on the device, but not the data on the server: erase it from the game first, or write to us.

Retention

Until you erase it, from the game or on simple request.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and asks for no personal information; it is not specifically aimed at children.

Your rights and contact

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure and objection (GDPR). To exercise these rights, or for any question: fongue.jeremy@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL.
